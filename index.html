GAMEPLAY EXPERIENCE QA

Gameplay Experience QA goes beyond finding bugs. I analyse how players understand, learn and experience a game — from the first interaction to long-term engagement.

Through structured playtesting, gameplay observation and detailed reporting, I turn real player behaviour into actionable feedback for developers.

I approach games as both a player and a tester, paying attention to gameplay clarity, onboarding, difficulty, progression, pacing, UX, player agency and unexpected interactions.

  • Puzzle & Narrative Design

  • Bugs & Unexpected Interactions
  • Systems & Gameplay Loops

HOW I APPROACH PLAYTESTING

I don't always follow the path the developer intended. I explore, experiment and test things that a real player might reasonably try — including unusual interactions, alternative approaches and unexpected combinations.

I pay attention to moments where the game communicates something clearly, where the player may become confused, and where systems produce unexpected or interesting behaviour.

When I discover a problem, I document what happened, why it may affect the player experience and, when relevant, how the issue can be reproduced.

HOW I WORK

PLAY → OBSERVE → INVESTIGATE → DOCUMENT → RECOMMEND

My reports focus on more than identifying what happened. I aim to explain the player experience behind the observation, why something may create friction or confusion, and what could potentially improve it.
