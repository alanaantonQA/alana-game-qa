HOW A PROJECT CAN START

Every project is different, so I am happy to adapt the scope to what the development team needs.

A project can begin with a focused playtest session addressing a specific question, or with a broader evaluation covering onboarding, gameplay, progression, UX and overall player experience.

For localization work, I can review the available text and project scope first and then provide an appropriate quote.

FREELANCE WORK

My standard freelance rate for Gameplay Experience QA and structured playtesting is approximately €25/hour.

Depending on the project, I can also work with a fixed price based on the agreed scope, playtime and reporting requirements.

Localization projects can likewise be quoted separately according to the amount of text, required LQA and overall scope.
